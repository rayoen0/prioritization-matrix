Faster input formats

Mix types in one list with a prefix: P: Slow approvals or I: Auto-approve under €500.

Paste straight from Excel (tab-separated) or use | to pre-score:
Name | Type | Feasibility | Value | Size | Colour
e.g. Delegate approvals | I | 9 | 6 | 1 | blue

Feasibility and value run 0–10, size 1–5. Colour is a palette name, a number 1–9, or one of your colour meanings (e.g. Finance). Bullets and numbering are stripped. A header row starting with “Name” is skipped.

New dots without scores land in the centre with a dashed ring until you drag them.
